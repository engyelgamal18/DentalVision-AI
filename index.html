About the Project

Exploring AI for Panoramic Dental X-rays

DentalVision AI is a hands-on project where I explored how artificial intelligence can be used with panoramic dental X-rays.

I worked with dental image data, annotations, model training, predictions, and evaluation. My goal was to understand how AI can support dental image review while keeping the dentist responsible for the final clinical decision.
