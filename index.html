About the Project

Building AI to Support Panoramic Dental X-ray Analysis

DentalVision AI is an artificial intelligence project developed to assist dentists in reviewing panoramic dental X-rays. The system analyzes images and highlights potential findings to support faster and more consistent clinical evaluation.

The project addresses the challenge of reviewing large numbers of panoramic X-rays by providing AI-assisted analysis that supports clinical decision-making while keeping the dentist responsible for the final diagnosis.

AI Workflow
